lstm & gru sequence models, trained in your browser

Teach a network to read the tape.

Lookback trains a recurrent neural network — your choice of LSTM or GRU — directly in this page, on real price history or a synthetic walk. No server, no API key: TensorFlow.js does the work on your device, then projects the series forward.
